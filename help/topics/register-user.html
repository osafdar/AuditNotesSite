Register User

When you open the truAudits application for the first time, you are required to register as a user. Registration can be started by clicking the Register button on the login screen.

This will open a popup where you must provide the following information:

  • Email address
  • Full name
  • Address
  • Phone number
  • Country
  • 4-digit PIN (required to retain access and for password changes)
Important: Please do not forget your 4-digit PIN. It is required to change your password and to maintain secure access to the application.

After entering your details, a verification code will be sent to your email address. Enter this code in the application to verify your email ownership and complete the registration process.

Privacy Notice:
Only your email, name, city, and country are saved on the truAudits server for future communication.
Your personal address is not saved on the server.
